1 → 700.

"The example of those who spend their wealth in the way of Allah is like a seed which grows seven ears; in each ear is a hundred grains. And Allah multiplies for whom He wills. And Allah is all-Encompassing and Knowing." — al-Baqarah 2:261

One seed. Seven ears. Seven hundred grains — and the verse doesn't stop at 700: "And Allah multiplies for whom He wills."

The Prophet ﷺ said that a good deed is written "from ten to seven hundred times, and many more" (al-Bukhārī 6491, Muslim 131).

And he ﷺ said: "Charity never decreased wealth." (Muslim 2588)
