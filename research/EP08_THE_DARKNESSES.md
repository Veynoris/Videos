**Wording discipline:**
- The "three darknesses" are the tafsir reading. They are labelled with "Ibn Kathīr on 21:87".
- "Three phrases lift three darknesses" exists only as a visual metaphor. The video never claims it.
- No people or creatures are drawn. Yūnus is a single point of light, and the great fish is only the enclosing darkness. The ship is an object.
