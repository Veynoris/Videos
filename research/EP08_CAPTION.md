# EP08 — caption

Three darknesses. One sentence got him out.

Yūnus left his people in anger. He boarded a laden ship, lots were cast, and he lost — and the great fish swallowed him. (21:87, 37:140–142)

"And he called out within the darknesses" — the darkness of the night, of the sea, and of the belly of the fish (Ibn Kathīr):

لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبۡحَٰنَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّـٰلِمِينَ
"There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers." (21:87)

He declared Allah's oneness. He glorified Him. He admitted his own fault.

"So We responded to him and saved him from the distress. And thus do We save the believers." (21:88)
— not only him.

The Prophet ﷺ said of this call: "No Muslim calls with it for anything, except that Allah responds to him." (al-Tirmidhī 3505)

Whatever darkness you're in tonight — say it. 🤍
