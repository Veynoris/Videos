**Wording discipline:**
- On screen the decoding is attributed to "Scholars of rhetoric", with the source shown.
- The scholars' verbs are set in Amiri (naskh), and the Qur'anic segments in KFGQPC.
- No ants, people or jinn are drawn. The army is rows of light, and the ants are moving points of light.
- The video gives no name for the ant and no location for the valley, since both are disputed.
