**Wording discipline:**
- The arithmetic shown (1 seed → 7 ears → 700 grains) is exactly the verse.
- The video does not promise that money multiplies 700×. The verse is about reward for spending in Allah's way, and the hadith covers the reward and the wealth.
- The Arabic of the hadith is set in Amiri (naskh) so it is never confused with Qur'anic text.
- "One seed. Hidden in the earth." and "What will you plant today?" are narration, not claims.
