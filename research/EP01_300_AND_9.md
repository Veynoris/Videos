## 1. The claim

| Layer | Content | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Qur'anic text | Al-Kahf 18:25 says "three hundred years" and then "and they added nine". It does not say "309". | Verified against the KFGQPC Hafs text |
| Arithmetic | 300 solar years = 109,572.7 days = **309.2 lunar years**. The difference from exactly 309 is about 73 days. | Computed (see §4) |
| Interpretation | Ibn Kathīr explains the "+9" as solar vs lunar reckoning: about 3 years per century. | Attributed; wording from search extracts (§3) |
| Disagreement | Qatāda (via al-Ṭabarī) held that the words report what people claimed, answered by "Say: Allah knows best…" in 18:26. | Shown on screen |

What the video does **not** claim:
- that the figure is "exact to the day" (it says plainly that it isn't);
- that this proves anything beyond what Ibn Kathīr says;
- any modern scientific miracle.

## 2. Qur'anic text

Text: KFGQPC Hafs ʿan ʿĀṣim (`quran-json@3.1.2`, from QuranEnc). Rendered in the KFGQPC Uthmanic Script Hafs font. English: Sahih International.

- **18:25** — وَلَبِثُواْ فِي كَهۡفِهِمۡ ثَلَٰثَ مِاْئَةٖ سِنِينَ وَٱزۡدَادُواْ تِسۡعٗا
  *"And they remained in their cave for three hundred years and exceeded by nine."*
- **18:26 (opening clause shown)** — قُلِ ٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا لَبِثُواْۖ
  *"Say, 'Allah is most knowing of how long they remained.'"*

Reading note: the video follows Ḥafṣ (ثَلَاثَ مِائَةٍ سِنِينَ with tanwīn). Ḥamza and al-Kisāʾī read it without tanwīn (ثَلَاثَ مِائَةِ سِنِينَ). The meaning is unaffected.

## 3. Sources

1. **Ibn Kathīr, *Tafsīr al-Qurʾān al-ʿAẓīm*, on al-Kahf 18:25.**
   - The search extract gives his explanation as: «…فإن تفاوت ما بين كل مائة سنة بالقمرية إلى الشمسية ثلاث سنين، فلهذا قال بعد الثلاثمائة: وازدادوا تسعا».
   - Online texts:
     - King Saud University: https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/katheer/sura18-aya25.html
     - Islamweb library, vol. 5: https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/49/1065/
   - ⚠ This environment's network blocks those sites, so the wording comes from search-engine extracts and has not been read on the page.
   - **Before publishing,** check the quote against the KSU page or a printed edition and add volume and page.
   - The on-screen English is our own translation and is labelled "translated".
2. **al-Ṭabarī, *Jāmiʿ al-Bayān*, on 18:25–26.**
   - Reports Qatāda's view that "and they added nine" relates what the People of the Book said.
   - Known to me through secondary search summaries; it needs the same check before publishing.
3. **Astronomical constants (standard values):**
   - mean tropical year 365.2422 days;
   - mean synodic month 29.530589 days, so 12 months = 354.367 days.

Ibn Kathīr died in 774 AH / 1373 CE, hence "over 650 years ago" (2026 − 1373 = 653).

## 4. Arithmetic (reproducible)

```
300 × 365.2422           = 109,572.7 days
109,572.7 ÷ 354.367      = 309.207 lunar years
309 × 354.367            = 109,499.4 days   → gap ≈ 73 days
per century: 100 × 365.2422 ÷ 354.367 − 100 = 3.07 extra lunar years
```

`video/src/lib/astro.ts` holds the same constants. The on-screen year grids and counters are driven by them frame by frame:
- the sun counter stops at exactly 300;
- the moon counter stops at 309 (floor of 309.2).

## 7. Sound design (v3: halal)

**Policy.**
- No music, no musical instruments, **no bells or chimes**, no melodies, chords or tonal pads, and no Qur'an recitation used as an effect.
- Many scholars hold that musical instruments are impermissible. Sahih Muslim 2114: *"The bell is the flute of Shayṭān."*
- Every sound is built from non-musical, natural sources:
  - air, wind and breath-like swells;
  - earth and stone thuds, pebbles and sand;
  - cave water drops;
  - paper and a mechanical clock tick.
- The spectrum shows no sustained tonal lines anywhere in the mix. The only pitched element is the brief natural chirp of the water drops.

**How it's built.**
- `video/scripts/sound.py` synthesises everything with NumPy/SciPy and processes it with Spotify Pedalboard.
- It uses synthetic hall and cave convolution spaces.
- Cues come from `src/timeline.json`, which is shared with the picture.

| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| "300" (f0) | Soft earth thud + a warm air swell (the sun) + sand trickling with the light sweep |
| "+9" (f24) | Reversed air swell into a lighter thud + a soft swirling air ring (rings are much quieter than v2) |
| Hook lines | Light air passes; a deeper soft thud on "One number. Two calendars." |
| Verse | Air lift as the card tilts up, parchment rustle, sand glints on the highlighted phrases |
| Cave | Cave wind, three water drops, a pebble, rising wind pressure, the push-through rush, open night air |
| Orbits | Two soft rotating-air textures (left/right); completions are released breaths and a gentle thud |
| Grid | Rising wind, a soft clock tick each decade, **nine pebble drops for the nine extra years**, a reversed swell into the main earth thud, sand sweep |
| Scholar / next verse | Quiet: parchment, low air, sand moving right→left with the Arabic |
| Outro | Rising wind, a soft deep thud at the sun–moon merge, the air settling into silence |

**Master.**
- Chain: high-pass 30 Hz → low-pass 16 kHz → high-shelf −2.5 dB at 7 kHz → gentle 1.8:1 compressor → limiter.
- Level: **−16 LUFS**, quieter than v2's −14, with a −2 dBFS ceiling.
- Reverb tails ring out naturally; no clicks were found in the spectrum.
