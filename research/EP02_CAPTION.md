# EP02 — caption

A mother was told to cast her baby into a river — and she obeyed.

The command came in one verse of 22 words (al-Qaṣaṣ 28:7):
"And We inspired to the mother of Moses: 'Suckle him; but when you fear for him, cast him into the river, and do not fear and do not grieve. Indeed, We will return him to you and will make him one of the messengers.'"

Al-Qurṭubī records that a Bedouin girl once told the great scholar al-Aṣmaʿī: in this single verse Allah gathered two commands, two prohibitions, two pieces of news and two glad tidings.

✦ Commands: suckle him · cast him into the river
✦ Prohibitions: do not fear · do not grieve
✦ News & glad tidings: We will return him to you · We will make him a messenger

Six verses later, the promise is kept: "So We restored him to his mother that she might be content and not grieve, and that she would know that the promise of Allah is true." (28:13)

Whatever you are holding on to today — the promise of Allah is true.
